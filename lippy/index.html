About

finding the right lip combo is really hard. when i find something i like in a store, i can only try on one product at a time, but the look i'm after is a liner, a lipstick and a gloss together, usually from three different brands.

lippy puts all three on your face at once. it reads your lips and skin through your camera and picks three combos for you: everyday, 90s brown and night out. you see them live, and you can turn the liner, lipstick and gloss on and off to see what each one adds.

the matching is skin-aware. lippy measures how light or deep your skin is and its undertone, so a tan or deep skin tone never gets a nude that washes it out. it picks from about 2,000 shades from 35 brands sold at sephora and ulta.

when you like a look, lock it in. it goes on your vanity with a picture of you wearing it, and every piece links to the brand's own store, so you check out once per brand. a lot of them run on shopify.

a few things i was strict about:

  • lippy detects your natural lip color. you never pick it yourself.
  • if you're already wearing lip color, it reads past it.
  • your camera stays on your device. nothing is uploaded.

try it on your phone. it takes about a second to read your lips.
